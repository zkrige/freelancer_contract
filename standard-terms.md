# Standard Terms of Business for Software Development Services

**Version 2.1**

These Standard Terms are incorporated by reference into the Project Terms signed by the Parties. They do not stand alone: they take effect only once Project Terms referring to them are signed.

Nothing in these Standard Terms is project specific. Every project variable (fees, rates, dates, scope, periods) lives in the Schedules to the Project Terms.

---

## 1. Interpretation and Definitions

### 1.1 Definitions

In the Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

**"Acceptance Period"** means the period stated in Schedule 1 during which the Customer may assess and test a Deliverable, extended as provided in clause 5.1.

**"Ad Hoc Project"** means an engagement with no fixed scope, in which the Customer directs work to the Developer on an ongoing basis and the Developer bills for time worked. The opposite of a Fixed Scope Project.

**"Agreement"** means, together: the Project Terms, the Schedules, these Standard Terms, and any Change Order accepted under clause 6.

**"Business Day"** means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the Republic of South Africa.

**"Change Order"** means a written record of an agreed change to the Specifications, Deliverables or Schedules, signed or confirmed in writing by both Parties.

**"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with the Agreement, whether or not marked as confidential, including the Customer's business, technical and financial information, the Source Code, and the terms of the Agreement. It excludes information that is or becomes public other than through breach of the Agreement, that the receiving Party already lawfully held, that the receiving Party independently develops, or that the receiving Party is required by law to disclose.

**"Customer"** means the party identified as such in the Project Terms. Also referred to in the Agreement as the "Licensee" or the "Client".

**"Deliverables"** means the items the Developer is to deliver, as set out in Schedule 2.

**"Developer"** means the party identified as such in the Project Terms.

**"Development Fee"** means the fee for development work, at the rate or amount stated in Schedule 1.

**"Effective Date"** means the date on which the last Party signs the Project Terms.

**"Early Termination Fee"** means the amount payable under clause 17.1(d), calculated at the percentage of Total Project Cost stated in Schedule 1.

**"Fixed Scope Project"** means an engagement where the scope is defined in Schedule 2 and quoted on by the Developer, and does not change for the duration of the Agreement other than by Change Order.

**"Intellectual Property Rights"** means all patents, copyright, design rights, trade marks, trade secrets, database rights and all other rights of a like nature, whether registered or unregistered, anywhere in the world.

**"Milestone"** means a point in the project at which an invoice becomes due, as set out in Schedule 3.

**"Party"** means the Customer or the Developer, and **"Parties"** means both.

**"Payment Period"** means the period stated in Schedule 1 within which an invoice must be paid.

**"Procured Items"** means the domain names, hosting, certificates, software licences, subscriptions, accounts, registrations and other third party products or services that the Developer acquires on the Customer's written instruction and for the Customer's benefit under clause 9A, as recorded in Schedule 4. Procured Items are not Software.

**"Procurement Fee"** means the handling fee, if any, stated in Schedule 1 for acquiring Procured Items.

**"Project Terms"** means the project specific agreement signed by the Parties which incorporates these Standard Terms by reference.

**"Project Type"** means whether the engagement is a Fixed Scope Project or an Ad Hoc Project, as stated in the Project Terms.

**"Remedy Period"** means the period stated in Schedule 1 within which a Party must remedy a material breach after receiving notice of it.

**"Schedules"** means the schedules attached to the Project Terms, being at minimum Schedule 1 (Commercial Terms), Schedule 2 (Scope and Deliverables), Schedule 3 (Milestones and Payment) and Schedule 4 (Procured Items).

**"Software"** means the software the Developer is to develop under the Agreement, as described in Schedule 2, including the Source Code and any documentation delivered with it.

**"Source Code"** means the human readable form of the Software, together with the build and configuration files needed to compile and run it.

**"Specifications"** means the specifications for the Software defined by the Customer under clause 3.1 and recorded in Schedule 2.

**"Standard Terms"** means this document.

**"Support Period"** means the initial period of support and maintenance stated in Schedule 1.

**"Support Rate"** means the hourly rate for support and maintenance stated in Schedule 1.

**"Total Project Cost"** means the total fees payable for the project as stated in Schedule 1 or, for an Ad Hoc Project, the total invoiced to date.

**"Training Rate"** means the hourly rate for training stated in Schedule 1.

**"Transfer Period"** means the period stated in Schedule 1 within which the Customer must pay for and take transfer of a Procured Item under clause 9A.8.

**"Warranty Period"** means the period stated in Schedule 1 during which the warranties in clause 11.2 apply.

### 1.2 Interpretation

In the Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise:

(a) the singular includes the plural and vice versa, and a reference to one gender includes the others;

(b) "including", "includes" and "in particular" are not words of limitation and are read as if followed by "without limitation";

(c) "in writing" includes email, and a reference to a signed document includes a document signed electronically;

(d) a reference to a clause is a reference to a clause of these Standard Terms, and a reference to a Schedule is a reference to a Schedule to the Project Terms;

(e) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation;

(f) a reference to a period of days means calendar days unless the period is expressed in Business Days;

(g) all amounts are in South African Rand and exclude VAT unless stated otherwise in Schedule 1;

(h) where a clause is expressed to apply to a Fixed Scope Project or an Ad Hoc Project only, it has no effect in an engagement of the other Project Type; and

(i) no rule of construction operates against the Party that drafted the Agreement.

### 1.3 Order of Precedence

If there is a conflict between the documents making up the Agreement, they take precedence in the following order:

(a) a Change Order, in respect of what it changes;

(b) the Project Terms;

(c) the Schedules; and

(d) these Standard Terms.

## 2. Development Services

2.1 The Customer engages the Developer, and the Developer agrees to perform services for the Customer to develop, deliver, support and maintain the Software in accordance with the Agreement.

2.2 The Customer agrees that only the Developer is contracted to develop the Software. If the Customer wishes to bring in other developers, this must be agreed in writing beforehand.

2.3 Failure to adhere to clause 2.2 is a material breach, and the Customer agrees to pay all outstanding fees owing to the Developer for work done.

---

## 3. Duties and Responsibilities

3.1 **Specifications.** The Customer shall define the Specifications and Deliverables, which are recorded in Schedule 2, and the delivery dates, which are recorded in Schedule 3.

3.2 **Development.** The Developer shall design, develop and implement the Software in accordance with the Specifications.

3.3 **Delivery.** The Developer shall use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver the Software to the Customer in accordance with the Specifications and Schedule 3.

3.4 **Responsibility for quality.** The Developer is responsible for ensuring that all necessary measures (code reviews, meetings, procedures and structures) are in place so that the quality and scope of the work meet the reasonable requirements expected by the Customer.

3.5 **Code commits.** Code will be committed to a git repository as often as reasonably possible to avoid loss. That repository will be the Developer's, and code will be pushed to a Customer repository only in accordance with clause 14.3.

---

## 4. Time Logging (Ad Hoc Projects only)

4.1 All time shall be logged against the correct project.

4.2 Time logs shall include a description of the work done in the logged time, in enough detail for the Customer to understand what was worked on.

---

## 5. Acceptance (Fixed Scope Projects only)

5.1 **Acceptance Period.** The Customer has the Acceptance Period following the date of delivery to assess and test the Software. If bugs are found, the Acceptance Period is extended by a further Acceptance Period running from the date the Developer commits the fix.

5.2 **Completion.** If the Developer delivers the Software in accordance with the Specifications, Deliverables and Schedule 3, the Developer is deemed to have completed its delivery obligations.

5.3 **Rejection.** If the Developer fails to deliver the Software in accordance with the Specifications, Deliverables or Schedule 3, then:

(a) **Notification.** The Customer shall detail in writing its grounds for rejection; and

(b) **Rectification.** The Developer shall use its best efforts to correct the Software, and on delivery of the correction the process of acceptance, completion and rejection restarts.

(c) **Continued failure.** If the Developer's corrections still fail to deliver the Software in accordance with the Specifications, Deliverables or Schedule 3, the Customer may elect to (i) terminate the Agreement, or (ii) adjust the Specifications, Deliverables or Schedule 3 by Change Order.

---

## 6. Change Orders (Fixed Scope Projects only)

6.1 **Changes.** The Customer may request changes to the Specifications, Deliverables or Schedules.

6.2 **Additional time or expense.** If the proposed change will, in the reasonable opinion of the Developer, require a delay in delivery of the Software or result in additional expense, then:

(a) the Parties shall confer; and

(b) the Customer may elect either to (i) withdraw its proposed change, or (ii) require the Developer to deliver the Software with the proposed change, subject to the delay and additional expense, recorded in a Change Order.

6.3 **Refusal.** If the Developer cannot or chooses not to accept the change order, the Customer may elect to terminate the Agreement. Termination under this clause is a termination by the Customer for no cause, and clauses 17.1(c) and 17.1(d) apply.

---

## 7. Training (Fixed Scope Projects only)

7.1 **Training scope.** Training is not included unless Schedule 2 says otherwise.

7.2 **Dates and locations.** Training will be conducted on such dates and at such locations as the Parties may agree.

7.3 **Training costs.** Training is charged at the Training Rate.

---

## 8. Support and Maintenance (Fixed Scope Projects only)

8.1 **Initial period.** The Developer shall provide the Customer with support and maintenance services for the Support Period following delivery and acceptance.

8.2 **Renewal.** The Customer may renew its maintenance and support subscription after the Support Period at the Developer's then current rates.

8.3 **Maintenance fees.** Support and maintenance are charged at the Support Rate. Support and maintenance fees are not payable for bug fixes.

---

## 9. Fees, Invoicing and Payment

9.1 **Development fees.** All development activities are charged as set out in Schedule 1. Rates in Schedule 1 are valid for the duration of the Agreement.

9.2 **Expenses.** The Customer will reimburse the Developer only for expenses agreed in writing by the Customer.

9.3 **Invoices.** For a Fixed Scope Project, invoices are raised on the Milestones set out in Schedule 3, which must be agreed and signed before the project starts. For an Ad Hoc Project, payment is cash on delivery and invoices are raised on the date of each month stated in Schedule 3.

9.4 **Payment.** All fees are due and payable in full to the Developer no later than the end of the Payment Period after the date of the Developer's invoice.

9.5 **Late fees.** Any amount not paid within the Payment Period bears interest at the rate stated in Schedule 1, or the highest rate allowable in law, whichever is greater.

9.6 **Recovery of fees.** All costs (legal and otherwise) incurred in recovering monies owed to the Developer are for the Customer's account.

---

## 9A. Procured Items

9A.1 **Instruction.** The Developer will acquire Procured Items only on the Customer's written instruction. Procured Items known at the Effective Date are listed in Schedule 4. A Procured Item acquired on a later written instruction is deemed added to Schedule 4 without a Change Order.

9A.2 **Cost.** Procured Items are charged at cost plus the Procurement Fee. A written instruction under clause 9A.1 is agreement in writing for the purposes of clause 9.2. The Developer may require payment before acquiring or renewing a Procured Item, and may otherwise include the cost in its next invoice under clause 9.3.

9A.3 **Ownership.** The Customer is the beneficial owner of each Procured Item from payment of its cost. Where a Procured Item is registered or held in the Developer's name, the Developer holds it as nominee for the Customer, will not use it for any other purpose, and will act on the Customer's reasonable instructions in respect of it. Clause 10 does not apply to Procured Items.

9A.4 **Transfer.** On the Customer's written request, and in any event on termination or expiry of the Agreement, the Developer will transfer each Procured Item to the Customer or its nominee, provided all amounts owing in respect of that Procured Item have been paid. The Customer bears any transfer fees and shall provide the account details needed to receive the transfer. Where the third party provider does not permit transfer, the Developer will cooperate reasonably so that the Customer can obtain an equivalent, and will cancel the Procured Item on the Customer's written instruction.

9A.5 **Renewals.** The Developer will give the Customer reasonable notice of any renewal date it is aware of. The Developer is not obliged to renew a Procured Item unless the Customer has instructed the renewal in writing and paid for it. The Developer is not liable for the lapse, loss or suspension of a Procured Item resulting from the Customer's failure to instruct or pay in time.

9A.6 **Third party terms.** The Customer shall comply with the third party provider's terms for each Procured Item. The Developer gives no warranty in respect of Procured Items, and clause 11.3 applies to them. The Customer shall indemnify the Developer against all claims, costs and liabilities arising from the Customer's use of a Procured Item or any content hosted on or through it.

9A.7 **Access.** Until transfer, the Developer may retain administrative control of a Procured Item and will give the Customer the access reasonably necessary for its use.

9A.8 **Unclaimed items.** If, within the Transfer Period after termination or expiry of the Agreement or after the Developer's written notice that a Procured Item is ready for transfer, the Customer has not paid all amounts owing in respect of that Procured Item or has not provided what is needed to receive its transfer, the Developer may cancel the Procured Item or allow it to lapse, without liability to the Customer.

## 13. Project Team

13.1 **Team lead.** The Developer will ensure that the Customer is provided with a competent team lead.

13.2 **English.** All team members must be proficient in English.

13.3 **Consistency.** The Developer will endeavour to provide the same project team to the Customer on each project engagement.

## 14. Confidentiality, Publicity and Source Code

14.1 **Confidentiality.** Each Party will use reasonable care to prevent the unauthorised use or dissemination of the other Party's Confidential Information, both during the term of the Agreement and after it ends.

14.2 **Publicity.** Unless agreed in writing with the Customer, no marketing or publicity featuring the Customer's projects may take place, including featuring the project in the Developer's portfolio or on the Developer's website.

14.3 **Source code (Fixed Scope Projects only).**

(a) **Delivery.** The Source Code developed under the Agreement is delivered to the Customer on payment of all outstanding monies.

(b) **Source control.** The Source Code is kept in the Developer's source control repository until final payment.

(c) **Customer repository.** If the Customer wishes to keep the Source Code in its own repository from the outset, the Total Project Cost must be paid to the Developer upfront as a deposit.

## 15. Project Management

15.1 The project team will attend the project meetings necessary during the course of the project. All meetings, telephone calls and travel are billed at the rates in Schedule 1 unless Schedule 1 provides otherwise. If these hours exceed the hours allowed for in Schedule 1, the excess is billed at the rates in Schedule 1.

15.2 The Developer will make itself available at reasonable notice.

## 16. Non-Solicitation

16.1 During the term of the Agreement and for the period stated in Schedule 1 after any termination of the Agreement, the Developer will not, directly or indirectly, on its own behalf or in the service or on behalf of others, in any capacity:

(a) **Solicit business.** solicit the business or patronage of any client of the Customer for any other person or entity;

(b) **Divert business.** divert, entice or otherwise take away from the Customer the business or patronage of any client of the Customer, or attempt to do so; or

(c) **Terminate business.** solicit or induce any client of the Customer to terminate or reduce its relationship with the Customer.

## 17. Rights and Remedies

17.1 **Termination.**

(a) **Termination for cause.** If a Party (i) commits a material breach or material default in the performance of any of its obligations under the Agreement, and (ii) that breach or default continues for the Remedy Period after the other Party delivers written notice reasonably detailing it, then (iii) the non-breaching Party may terminate the Agreement with immediate effect by written notice.

(b) **Effect of termination.** On termination of the Agreement: (i) any licence granted to the Customer under the Agreement terminates; and (ii) the Customer shall cease all use of the Software and destroy or erase all copies of the Software in its possession or control. This clause does not apply to Procured Items, which are dealt with under clause 9A.4.

(c) **Outstanding fees.** On termination of the Agreement, all monies owing to the Developer become immediately payable.

(d) **Termination for no cause.** If the Customer terminates the Agreement early for no good cause, including under clause 6.3, the Early Termination Fee is payable to the Developer in addition to the amounts due under clause 17.1(c).

17.2 **Indemnification.** The Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold the Developer harmless from and against all costs, charges and expenses (including legal fees) arising from any third party claim, action, suit or proceeding alleging that the Software, or any services performed under the Agreement, infringes a patent, copyright or other Intellectual Property Right or violates a trade secret.

17.3 **Limitation of liability.** In no event will the Developer be liable for any direct, indirect, consequential, incidental, special, exemplary or punitive damages or liabilities arising from or relating to the Software, its content or the Agreement, whether based on contract, delict (including negligence), strict liability or any other theory, even if the Developer has been advised of the possibility of such damages.

## 18. General Provisions

18.1 **Term.** The Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all of the obligations of the Parties have been performed or until earlier terminated in accordance with the Agreement.

18.2 **Entire agreement.** The Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, representations and understandings, written or oral.

18.3 **Amendment.** The Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. A Change Order under clause 6 is such an instrument.

18.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign the Agreement or any of its rights under it without the prior written consent of the other Party, and any attempted assignment without that consent is void.

18.5 **Notices.**

(a) **Form.** All notices, requests, claims, demands and other communications between the Parties shall be in writing.

(b) **Method.** All notices shall be given (i) by delivery in person, (ii) by a recognised next day courier service, (iii) by registered mail, postage prepaid, or (iv) by email to the address of the Party specified in the Project Terms or such other address as either Party may specify in writing.

(c) **Receipt.** All notices are effective on (i) receipt by the Party to which the notice is given, or (ii) the fifth day following posting, whichever occurs first.

18.6 **Governing law.** The Agreement is governed, construed and enforced in accordance with the laws of the Republic of South Africa, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

18.7 **Severability.** If any provision of the Agreement is held by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the remainder of the Agreement remains in full force and effect.

18.8 **Waiver.** Failure or neglect by either Party to enforce at any time any of the provisions of the Agreement is not a waiver of that Party's rights under it.

18.9 **Survival.** Clauses 1, 9, 9A, 10, 11.3, 14, 16, 17.2, 17.3 and 18 survive termination or expiry of the Agreement.
